Digital Security Topics

Learn the most important ways to protect yourself online.

01

Password Security

Learn how to create strong passwords and keep your accounts protected.

  • Use long passwords
  • Do not reuse passwords
  • Use a password manager
02

Phishing

Understand how attackers use fake emails and messages to steal your information.

  • Check the sender
  • Do not trust unknown links
  • Never share sensitive data
03

Two-Factor Authentication

Add another layer of protection to your online accounts.

  • Use an authenticator app
  • Enable 2FA when possible
  • Protect your backup codes
04

Social Engineering

Learn how attackers manipulate people into revealing private information.

  • Be careful with urgent requests
  • Verify identities
  • Do not act under pressure
05

Safe Browsing

Browse the internet safely and avoid dangerous websites and downloads.

  • Check website addresses
  • Update your browser
  • Avoid unknown downloads
